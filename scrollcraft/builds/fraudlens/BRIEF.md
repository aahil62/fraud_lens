# FraudLens: the incident journey

Self-authored under the user's broad creative direction to replace the existing UI using Scrollcraft and Three.js. This records implementation decisions, not invented interview answers.

## Evidence and authority
The user rejected the previous interface and requested an improved landing page and every application page. The project is a synthetic financial crime operations showcase. It supports multiple payment channels, recorded decisions, graph evidence and a separate bank simulator. It has no external bank connection. Product/source/API contracts are preserved.

## The eight topics
1. Vibe: precise, lucid, dimensional, considered. Authored references: optical instrumentation and the layout hierarchy of a technical journal. These are not user-supplied preferences.
2. Journey: see signal become legible; choose a payment rail; trace one illustrative incident; enter the operational workspace.
3. Energy: confident dimensional opening; quiet channel inspection; evidence becomes the interactive peak; a calm resolved handover.
4. Feelings: curiosity, orientation, understanding, agency. The memorable moment is changing an incident step and seeing exactly what the protection acknowledgement and analyst verdict mean.
5. Signature: a real optical instrument routes four payment streams, with the chosen channel carrying into the sample incident. The visitor can trace its evidence, simulated protection and decision rather than watching a passive animation.
6. Aesthetic range: authored precise mineral/cobalt technical editorial, not decorative luxury or terminal aesthetics.
7. Distinct scenes. No continuous camera flight. This is an incident journey, where each section answers the previous section's next question.
8. Assets: existing source and product data conventions. All scene geometry is procedural Three.js; no paid image/video generation or external assets.

## Belief and action
Belief: a suspicious transaction is useful only when its evidence, protection state and decision remain connected.
Action: **Open workspace**, linking to `/login` everywhere.

## Grammar
Custom incident journey. Navigation: in-flow brand and anchor links to the payment rails and the example. Sequence: dimensional instrument → quiet payment selector → operable incident dossier → plain typographic handover. Ending: decision line connects into the login action. Bans: fake KPIs, client logos, broad performance claims, full-screen pinned scroll taxes, hidden copy, floating dashboard cards, extra CTA slabs.

Other grammars considered: filmic one-shot adds passive media and obscures the actual decision; chaptered editorial hides the requested 3D opening; live surface forbids marketing typography; continuous world introduces unnecessary camera geography; catalogue repeats cards; field guide implies a long reference; poster has insufficient product substance; timeline implies historical chronology. The incident journey preserves useful operation while keeping a deliberate authored opening.

## Feeling curve (written before score)
- Opening: curiosity. The machined glass-and-cobalt lens makes payment signals tangible.
- Rails: orientation. Four real channel categories become a quiet tactile selector.
- Incident: understanding. One selected sample advances through evidence, protection and analyst decision. This is the longest section.
- Close: agency. The incident line resolves into a concrete workspace entry.

## Peak and memory hook
Peak, incident act: “I changed the payment rail and could follow the suspicious payment all the way to its recorded decision.”
Tell-someone sentence: “It's the site where the payment streams go through a lens, and I can follow one through the investigation myself.”
No empty scroll or authored silence. The rail section is visually quiet before the incident peak.

## Scroll score
| Beat | Device family | Implementation | Scroll budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| See the signal | Dimensional parallax | Three.js independent optical layers and pointer response; Scrollcraft flow progress moves a decorative background word | about 1 viewport |
| Choose the rail | Direct manipulation | Accessible tab buttons update channel explanation and sample incident | about 0.6 viewport |
| Trace the incident | Staged dossier | Three labeled stages update meaningful sample evidence and resolution; sticky supporting column on desktop only | about 1.25 viewports |
| Enter the workspace | Typographic resolution | Horizontal rule resolves into large inline entry action | about 0.5 viewport |

No two adjacent device families repeat. No essential content uses scroll reveals. Reduced motion retains complete static SVG optics and operable direct controls. The unchanged engine is archived beside this brief and used from the React client boundary; no independent HTML app or iframe.
