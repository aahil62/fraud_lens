# Product

## Register

product

## Users

FraudLens is a local portfolio showcase for evaluators and learners exploring a fraud analyst's workflow. An analyst investigates fictional suspicious payments, records a reasoned verdict, and follows protective actions through a separate enforcing bank simulator. Four channels are included: UPI, net banking, cards and ATMs.

## Product purpose

Connect detection evidence, case review, protective requests, bank acknowledgements and incident history in one consistent ledger. Moderate risk opens review; supported high-risk patterns request simulated restrictions automatically. Analysts can confirm fraud or reverse a false positive; release acknowledgement determines final resolution. The current operational policy is versioned separately from the preserved historical benchmark components.

## Brand and design

A precise optical instrument in a bank vault's control room: warm graphite, stone work surfaces, one brass accent and retro ledger type. The landing page demonstrates the idea through a dimensional lens and an interactive, clearly labelled sample incident. The console prioritizes reading, state clarity and useful density. See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for the current visual system and [docs/history/UI-REDESIGN.md](docs/history/UI-REDESIGN.md) for the overhaul's design decisions.

## Principles

- Every operational figure comes from the shared authoritative snapshot or an explicitly labelled bounded sample.
- Pending protection and confirmed enforcement are visibly different.
- Decisions retain actor, rationale, version and timeline.
- Marketing examples stay labelled as samples and do not mutate real operational records.
- Motion explains the product on the landing page; working screens use fast state feedback.
- Keyboard use, contrast, responsive layouts and reduced motion are part of implementation.

## Evidence boundary

No real bank connection, arbitrary-fraud detection, losses prevented or regulatory certification is claimed. NPCI/RBI applicability, partial source retrieval and external obligations are recorded in [docs/GUIDANCE.md](docs/GUIDANCE.md). Local and constructed checks are described in [docs/VERIFICATION.md](docs/VERIFICATION.md).
